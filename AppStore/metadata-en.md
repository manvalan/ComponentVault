Add parts by pointing the camera at the bag label, always know which drawer they're in, and check your project BOM at a glance.

**Description**:
ComponentVault is the parts store for people who design electronics. Keep resistors, capacitors, ICs and modules in order and always know what you have, where it is and what's missing for your next project.

LOAD FROM LABEL
Point the camera at the QR code of LCSC/JLCPCB bags or the DataMatrix of distributor labels: code, MPN and quantity fill in by themselves. Set the storage unit and drawer number and move to the next bag. On the Mac it also works with a USB barcode scanner.

COMPLETE PART PAGES
Description, parameters and datasheet from your archive; live prices and availability from Mouser and DigiKey with your own API keys.

PROJECTS AS CHECKLISTS
Import the BOM from EasyEDA/JLC and watch three rings: stock, KiCad library and prices. Tap a ring to see only what's missing, then order, reserve stock or download to KiCad with a single button.

KICAD LIBRARY
See which BOM parts are missing from your library and request them from the Mac that hosts it. Symbols, footprints and 3D models arrive checked, with a render to verify pin 1.

YOUR DATA, YOUR DEVICES
No account and no server. The database lives on your device. To use the same data on iPad and Mac, choose the same folder on both, for example in iCloud Drive: configuration, inventory and projects live there.

In English and Italian.
